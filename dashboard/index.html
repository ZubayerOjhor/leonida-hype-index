The whole market

The S&P 500, 500 big US companies together. On a day when every share went up, Take-Two going up isn't GTA news. So we always ask: did Take-Two do better or worse than the market?

How do you measure hype? We count how many people read the GTA VI page on Wikipedia each day, and how often people searched "GTA 6" on Google. More readers and searches = more people curious about the game.

How we know

Where the numbers come from

Data (all free and public)

  • Sales: Sensor Tower estimates, as reported by the BBC, The Business Standard and GameSpot. Take-Two hasn't published official GTA VI sales yet.
  • GTA V's sales history: Take-Two's reported totals (copies shipped), from 11 million on day one in 2013 to over 230 million in 2026.
  • Interest: daily readers of the English Wikipedia page "Grand Theft Auto VI" (live since Trailer 1 in Dec 2023).
  • Share prices: Take-Two (TTWO) and the S&P 500 fund (SPY), daily, from Yahoo Finance.
  • Searches: Google Trends for "GTA 6", worldwide and Bangladesh.

Things to keep in mind

  • Sales figures are estimates and they change: the launch-day estimate was $1.84B, a day later it was $2.36B.
  • The 2026–2035 numbers are scenarios built on GTA V's history, not a forecast anyone can promise.
  • Only about ten big GTA moments, so treat the stock averages as a story, not a law.
  • Fan project. Not linked to Rockstar Games or Take-Two. Not investment advice.
For the data-curious: the methods and terms

Methods

  • 2026–2035 scenarios. Day one = Sensor Tower's ~24.5M copies / ~$2.36B. After that, GTA VI adds GTA V's post-day-one copies at the same age (interpolated between Take-Two milestones), times 0.6 (low), 1.0 (middle) or 1.4 (high). Average money per copy is an assumption that falls from ~$80 in late 2026 to ~$30 from 2033, as discounts and cheaper editions take over. Calendar years; 2026 includes day one.
  • "Vs the market" is the abnormal return: Take-Two's daily return minus what a market model (TTWO regressed on SPY) predicts, fitted on trading days −130 to −10 before each event. News after the US close counts on the next trading day.
  • Trading and interest (× normal). That day's volume ÷ median of the previous 60 trading days; that day's Wikipedia views ÷ median of the previous 30 days.
  • Section "Busy, not up" correlates daily surprises (log value minus its trailing baseline), because raw series trend over time and mislead.
  • Wanted level. 20-day volatility of Take-Two's daily returns, ranked against its own 2023–26 history: 5 stars = top 5%.

Terms and numbers

  • r (correlation): how closely two things move together, from 0 (not at all) to 1 (perfectly). Under 0.1 none, 0.1–0.3 weak, 0.3–0.5 moderate, above 0.5 strong.
  • Sold-in: copies shipped to shops and digital stores, the figure Take-Two reports.
